Getting Started

Three steps to production

1

Get Your Workspace

Sign in with GitHub, choose a team name, and get your isolated namespace with RBAC and secrets — instantly.

Sign In → Create Team → Get Namespace
↓
2

Configure Your Service

Pick a template from the Service Catalog. Specify database, domain, and secrets. Repo and configs generated automatically.

Choose Template → Configure → Generate
↓
3

Push and Deploy

Push your code. The platform builds, tests, and deploys to Kubernetes automatically via GitOps.

git push → Build → Deploy → Live ✓
